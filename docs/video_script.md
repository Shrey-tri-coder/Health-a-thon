# Healtheon demo video script (about 2:30)

**0:00 Problem (voiceover, title slide)**
"Before every cancer consultation, oncologists spend around 14 minutes or more hunting through folders, EMRs and PDFs. Healtheon gives them that picture before the patient walks in."

**0:20 Patient app (screen: Patient tab, P001)**
"Between visits, the patient fills a short symptom and missed-medication form. They can also upload a report by choosing its type. It is saved straight into their folder with date and time."

**0:50 Staff app (Staff tab)**
"Labs and radiology use the same upload screen. Staff pick the patient and document type. Nothing needs AI sorting because the type is chosen at upload. The session list flags two departments booked at the same time."

**1:15 Doctor, follow-up (Doctor tab, Follow-up)**
"The doctor scans the patient's QR code and picks the visit type. For a follow-up, they get a one-page brief: symptoms reported since the last visit, missed medications and new reports."

**1:40 Doctor, new patient and transfer (switch to P002, then P003)**
"A new patient gets a list of lab reports on file. A hospital transfer gets the full chronological history. Here the prior-hospital records are simulated, in place of ABDM exchange."

**2:05 Search (Search box)**
"If the doctor needs one report, they search by type and date."

**2:15 Close**
"AI is used only for the final summary, on demand, so cost stays low. It lists facts and counts only. No diagnosis, no risk scoring. Healtheon."
